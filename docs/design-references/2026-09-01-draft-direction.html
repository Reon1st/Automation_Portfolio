Type

Display: Archivo Expanded, bold uppercase, condensed/industrial feel — headline only.
Body: system sans, neutral.
Utility/data: Consolas / monospace — title-block stamp, node labels.

Layout

Single column. Opens with a technical title-block stamp (name/role/base/status as a 4-column mono grid) instead of a bio line. Section transitions are routed circuit traces with right-angle bends and solder-point dots, not decorative hairlines.

Signature

Breaks the one convention nearly every AI-consultant portfolio shares — it isn't dark mode. Warm paper background, technical drafting register, traces that literally connect the named systems (echoes the site's real WorkflowEngine diagrams).
